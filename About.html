About Us

NEHRU VIKAS MANAV SAMITI is a non-profit organization working towards social upliftment, education, women empowerment, healthcare, and skill development since 1981. With a strong commitment to helping underprivileged and marginalized communities, especially in the rural and semi-urban areas of Rajasthan, the organization has touched and improved the lives of nearly 15,000 people through its continuous efforts and community programs. The organization runs Nehru Vidya Mandir Senior Secondary School in Kaman, District Deeg, Rajasthan, affiliated with the Rajasthan State Board. The school provides education to around 200 economically disadvantaged children, many of whom are first-generation learners. Along with quality education, the focus is also on moral values, life skills, confidence, and overall personality development to help children build a brighter future. NEHRU VIKAS MANAV SAMITI also works actively towards women empowerment by encouraging education, awareness, and self-reliance among women, helping them become confident and independent members of society. In addition, the organization promotes health and hygiene awareness and regularly organizes health camps to provide basic healthcare support and spread awareness in underserved communities. Through years of dedicated work, the organization continues to create positive change by giving hope, opportunities, and support to those who need it most.

Our Vision

To build a society where every child can learn, every woman feels strong and independent, and every person has access to basic health and care. We dream of a future where no one is left behind, and everyone can live with dignity, hope, and equal opportunities.
